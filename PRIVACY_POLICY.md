# Privacy Policy — Shift & Pay Tracker

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Shift & Pay Tracker is a free, fully offline shift and pay tracker for
individual hourly workers. This policy explains what the app does — and
does not do — with your information.

## Data stored on your device

All shift, job, template and deduction data you enter is stored **only on
your device**, in a local database. It is never uploaded, synced or shared
by the app itself. There is no account, no login and no server.

Settings (mileage rate, default job, currency) are stored locally on your
device using Android DataStore.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads
SDK). AdMob may collect device identifiers and other information as
described in Google's privacy policy in order to serve and measure ads.
On first launch the app asks for your consent through Google's UMP consent
form where required by law, and you can change your choices at any time
from the consent form.

The app does **not** collect your name, address, Social Security number,
bank details, precise location or contacts, and it does not ask for them.

## Data Safety (Play Console)

When filling in the Play Console Data Safety form, declare:

- Data collected by the app itself: **none** (all records stay on-device).
- Data collected by third-party SDKs: AdMob may collect device / advertising
  identifiers for advertising purposes — declare this as collected by the
  AdMob SDK, not by the app.
- Data is not sold and not shared with third parties by the app itself.

## Children's privacy

The app is intended for adults who work hourly jobs. It is not directed
at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the
app release that introduces the change.
